# Part VI — Renewable Energy and Electric Mobility

Part V built machines that consume electrical energy under control. This part builds the systems that produce, store and move it: a rooftop PV plant, the battery that stores its output, the electric vehicle that carries a battery on wheels, and the microgrid that makes PV, wind, storage, generators and the grid work as one. Almost every number in them is set by an environment the designer does not control — the coldest dawn of the decade, the hottest afternoon on a roof, a −10 °C delivery round, a feeder that trips at noon on a Sunday. Each chapter writes those extremes into its requirements, sizes strings, cells, converters and cables against them, budgets energy, voltage, temperature and current, and closes with tests against numbers fixed before the build.

## By the end of this part you should understand

- How a PV system is sized from irradiation data to the grid connection — string length at the coldest and hottest cell temperatures, inverter and MPPT choices, cables and protection — and how a loss tree predicts its yield and performance ratio.
- How a PV system is commissioned and monitored, and how a temperature-corrected performance ratio locates a fault.
- How a battery pack is architected from cell to rack against a converter's voltage window, with contactors, precharge, fuses and insulation monitoring.
- How a BMS measures, protects, balances and estimates state, and why its error budgets usually decide whether a battery meets its contract.
- How thermal design, charging limits, degradation and safety testing (IEC 62619, UL 9540A, UN 38.3) shape a battery's life.
- How an EV powertrain is sized from a duty cycle, and why cold weather and charging infrastructure belong in the first energy budget.
- How PV, wind, storage and generators are dispatched, protected and coordinated so that a site runs grid-connected and islanded, and how such a system is evaluated for reliability and cost.

## How this part connects to the rest of the book

The chapters build on each other: Chapter 22's school system contains a battery that Chapter 23 opens; Chapter 23's pack and BMS reappear on wheels in Chapter 24 and as the storage of Chapter 25's microgrid, which combines both under one energy-management system. All four use the method of Part I, the current and voltage measurement of Part II, the embedded firmware and gateways of Part III, and the CAN, Modbus and functional-safety practice of Chapter 21. Part IX applies them end to end: the solar battery project of Chapter 36 uses Chapters 22, 23 and 25, and the smart energy monitor of Chapter 35 measures such systems.

The theory is cited from the sibling books, not repeated. *Electrical Energy and Machines* owns renewable generation ([Ch. 15](https://github.com/The-Studio-of-Sadiq/Electrical-Energy-and-Machines/blob/main/part-4-power-generation/15-renewable-energy-systems.md)), storage and BMS functions ([Ch. 16](https://github.com/The-Studio-of-Sadiq/Electrical-Energy-and-Machines/blob/main/part-4-power-generation/16-energy-storage.md)), drive control ([Ch. 12](https://github.com/The-Studio-of-Sadiq/Electrical-Energy-and-Machines/blob/main/part-3-motor-drives/12-ac-motor-drives.md)), microgrids and grid integration ([Ch. 26–27](https://github.com/The-Studio-of-Sadiq/Electrical-Energy-and-Machines/blob/main/part-7-power-quality-and-grid-modernization/27-grid-integration-of-renewable-energy.md)) and electrical safety and cable design ([Ch. 29–30](https://github.com/The-Studio-of-Sadiq/Electrical-Energy-and-Machines/blob/main/part-8-electrical-safety-and-engineering-practice/30-electrical-system-design.md)). *Electronics and Computing* owns the converters, chargers and traction power stages ([Ch. 30–32](https://github.com/The-Studio-of-Sadiq/Electronics-and-Computing/blob/main/part-7-power-electronics/32-motor-drives-and-power-conversion.md)). *Engineering Physics* supplies solar-cell physics and thermal networks ([Ch. 14](https://github.com/The-Studio-of-Sadiq/Engineering-Physics/blob/main/CHAPTERS/CHAPTER%2014.md)), and *Materials Science and Engineering* PV and battery materials ([Ch. 40](https://github.com/The-Studio-of-Sadiq/Materials-Science-and-Engineering/blob/main/Chapters/CHAPTER%2040.md)).
