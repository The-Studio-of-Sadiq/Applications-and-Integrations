# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want *Applications and Integrations* to be a welcoming place to learn and to teach engineering design and systems integration. Everyone is welcome to take part, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being patient with people who are still learning. Every engineer once asked why a transistor needs biasing or what a pointer really is.
- Pointing out errors in the work, not in the person: "§7.5 uses the wrong sign for the common-emitter gain" rather than "you don't understand amplifiers".
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Crediting other people's ideas, figures, data and sources.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory, demeaning or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Plagiarism, or knowingly submitting copyrighted material (textbook passages, datasheet figures, standards text) without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or classroom setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki, commit messages, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers at **princekhan7748@gmail.com**. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will clarify and enforce these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity, consequences range from a private warning, to a temporary ban from interaction, to a permanent ban from the project.
